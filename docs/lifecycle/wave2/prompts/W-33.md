## Goal
Finance is told, N days before an `active` proposal ends, that its agreement still has unspent funds, and Public Relations gets a "Request extension" shortcut that opens W-08's modal (D-60). Money holders only ever see the fact "funds remain", never the amount, in the notification.

## Rules
- Item added in `DeadlineCatalog` (one method, like the others): active proposals whose agreement remaining amount is **> 0** (computed by `AgreementMoney`, in one query over the candidates, not per row) and whose effective end is within the lead window. No proposal without an agreement.
- The notification text: "{proposal} ends on {date} and its agreement still has funds." No figure, no currency, in either language.
- Settings page: the new number field next to the other lead-day fields, validated 1–365.
- Quick action label "Request extension" / «طلب تمديد»; the proposal page opens W-08's modal when `?extend=1` and the user may extend, else ignores the key.
