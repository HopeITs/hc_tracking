## Goal
A **payroll** proposal (D-55b) funds a center's volunteer salaries and equipment: narrative, donor, begin/end, attachment, a budget that Finance enters before it is sent, and a **final report only**. It has no projects, no logframe, no targets, no monthly reports, and it is invisible to MEAL aggregations. Its path is `proposal draft → submitted → approved → active → completed`.
