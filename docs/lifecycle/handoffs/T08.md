- Frozen `via` values: `proposal_approval`, `owner_acknowledgement` (approval); `template_confirmation` (activation). Anything else throws `InvalidArgumentException`.
- Policies: `ProposalPolicy::approve` (= `proposals.update`, checked on the locked row), `ProjectPolicy::activate` (= `report-templates.manage`), `AssignmentPolicy::approveProject` (acknowledgement path).
- Acknowledgement hook: `Project::assignmentAcknowledged(string $field)` calls `ApproveProjectFromProposal` with `owner_acknowledgement` in the acknowledging transaction (page `submitAssignment`, `QuickActions::runAssignment`, self-assignment, re-ask after `unblock()`).
- Events, all `ShouldDispatchAfterCommit`:
  - `ProposalStatusChanged`: dispatched in `ApproveProposal` (submitted/on hold -> approved) and once per proposal in `ActivateProject` (approved -> active).
  - `ProjectPeriodChanged`: dispatched in `ApproveProjectFromProposal::extendDates`, only for an `active` project whose dates grew.
  - `ProjectActivated(projectId, earliest approved proposal id|null, start, end, via)`: dispatched once at the end of `ActivateProject`.
- Activity keys: `proposal.approved`, `proposal.approved_without_agreement`, `project.approved` (context `via`, `proposal_id`), `project.owner_acknowledged`, `project.owner_declined`, `project.period_changed`, `project.activated`.
