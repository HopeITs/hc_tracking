### Policy abilities
**`MilestonePolicy`**
- `planForProposal(User, Proposal, ?Milestone = null): Response`:
  - Planning = `proposals.update`, or `milestones.create` + `view` on the proposal.
  - Execution = `milestones.create` (new) or `update` (existing).
  - Locked and Closed are refused with `milestones.plan_window_locked` / `milestones.plan_window_closed`.

**`TaskPolicy`**
- `planDepartmentTask(User, Proposal, ?Task = null): Response`: the same window logic, with `tasks.create` and `TaskPolicy::update`.
- `assignDepartmentTask(User, Task, Proposal): bool`: `tasks.update`, or a member of the task's department, while the proposal is not Closed (D-33).
- `deliver(User, Task): bool`: a media deliverable, and the user is its assignee, a member of its department, or someone who may edit it.
- `review(User, Task): bool`: a media deliverable, and the user is its creator or holds `proposals.update`.
- Department work (`update`) now also counts `tasks.department_id` = the user's HR department.

### Visibility (`TaskVisibilityScope::constrain()`; `allows()` reuses it)
These rules apply to department-scoped users only. Bypass and unscoped users are unchanged.
- Added to the existing OR group:
  - `tasks.department_id = user's HR department`;
  - for holders of `proposals.update`: `tasks.department_id IS NOT NULL`, so department tasks only and no pre-T31 row.
- Outside the group, `AND tasks.kind != 'financial'` applies unless the user holds the `Financial` role or `proposals.update` (Finance only; Media never).

**Common to all actions**
- Each one locks the proposal (plan actions) or the task (delivery actions) with `lockForUpdate`, inside one transaction.
- Children are loaded only through their parent: `$proposal->milestones()->whereKey()`, `$proposal->planTasks()->whereKey()`. A foreign id gives `ModelNotFoundException`, and a foreign parent milestone gives a `ValidationException`; nothing is written in either case.

**Plan rules**
- Period: actual dates when set, else planned. A start before the period or an end after it is refused with `milestones.milestone_outside_period`.
- A center-specific milestone's `project_id` must be one of the proposal's linked projects.
- Department tasks:
  - The department must exist in HR.
  - The assignee must be in that department (`EmployeeProfile::employeeIdsInDepartment`).
  - `due_date` must be on or after `requested_on`, which defaults to today.
  - `media_kinds` is required for media deliverables.
  - A financial title may not contain a number of 3+ digits, USD/EUR/SYP, `$`, `€` or `ل.س`.

**Delivery**

| Move | From state | Needs | Task status after |
|---|---|---|---|
| deliver | requested, returned | `delivered_on` ≤ today, plus evidence per kind (below) | `under review` |
| accept | delivered | — | `done`; also sets `completed_date`, progress 100 and `accepted_at` |
| return | delivered | a reason | `in progress`; reason saved in `returned_reason` |

- Evidence per kind:
  - video: a link only;
  - photos: jpg/png files or a link;
  - file: any `FileType` or a link;
  - text: text.
- Files:
  - `SecureUpload`, max 5 MB, at most 10 per delivery;
  - stored as `task_attachments` through `StoredFiles::save()`, encrypted;
  - downloaded through the existing `tasks.files.download` route (`throttle:files`).
- A state that doesn't match `expectedState` throws `department_tasks.stale_delivery_state` and writes nothing.
- `returned_reason` stays when the task is delivered again; accepting clears it.

**`DeleteProposalPlan`**
- Deletes every plan task, ignoring visibility, subtasks first.
- Removes attachment files from disk after the commit.
- Then deletes the milestones, deepest first, through Eloquent.

### Listener and ledger
`App\Listeners\RebaseProposalPlanOnApproval`
- `ShouldQueue`, `$tries = 3`, `$backoff = [10, 60, 300]`.
- `shouldQueue()` only queues events with `to = approved`.
- Reloads the proposal and its latest approved status note, then calls `RebaseProposalPlan`.
- A failure is reported and the job fails, so it never reaches the approver's request under the sync queue. On a real worker it is retried.

`RebaseProposalPlan` (system only: no user, no `Gate`):
1. Locks the proposal; skips if the ledger already holds the note.
2. Delta = `actual_date_begin − planned_date_begin` − Σ earlier `delta_days` (deviation 2).
3. Moves the planned dates of the proposal's milestones and plan tasks. Rows with `planned_dates_edited_at` set are skipped. Dates are written through the query builder, so they are not stamped as user edits.
4. Clips any date past `actual_date_end` to it.
5. Writes one ledger row and one activity `proposal.plan_rebased`:
   - `actor_id = null`;
   - properties: `delta_days`, `shifted`, `clipped_milestones`, `clipped_tasks` (each `[{id, name}]`), `value` (the clipped count), `project_id`.
6. A unique-key race gives `skipped`.
7. Skips without writing anything when the proposal has no actual dates.

## Deviations from contracts.md / the prompt
1. **New catalog key `proposal.plan_rebased`.** It is not in §15.2; the prompt asks to notify the owner with the clipped list but names no key. It was added under §15's "owning task adds it" rule. **Dev 3 to add the §15.2 row; Dev 1 to confirm.**
2. **Delta on re-approval.** `RebaseProposalPlan` subtracts the shifts already recorded for the proposal. A corrected-and-re-approved proposal therefore never moves its plan twice. A first approval is exactly `actual − planned`. **Dev 1 to confirm.**
3. **Project owners are exempt from `proposals.view` for `proposal.plan_rebased`.** Owners with the Managers role don't hold it. This is the same pattern as T06's MEAL exemption for `beneficiaries_changed`. **Dev 1 to confirm.**
4. **"Finance" = the `Financial` role** for financial-task visibility, and "proposal-rights holders" = `proposals.update`, limited to department tasks (plan Q2/Q3). **Dev 1 to confirm.**
5. **`planned_dates_edited_at` is stamped on any user edit after creation,** as §12.2 says literally. Dates edited during planning therefore do not shift at approval. **Dev 1 to confirm this is the intended reading of D-34.**
6. **`RebaseProposalPlan` has no `Gate::authorize`.** It is system-only, runs from the queue, and lives in `app/Actions/ProposalPlan/`, outside `ActionAuthorizationTest`'s scanned directories.
7. **`milestones.responsible_user_id` stays NOT NULL,** so a proposal milestone requires a responsible employee.
8. **`ProposalPlanShift` records no activity** (it's bookkeeping); the movement is logged on the proposal.
