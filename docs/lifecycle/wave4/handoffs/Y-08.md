## Deviations from contracts.md / the prompt
1. **Annual-plan targets whose project has no center** take their center from every location row that carries the project, when those rows agree. That is how `main` placed them. The prompt said "from the project only", which would have dropped the line `ReportTemplatePrefillTest` expects (a Stop rule). Approved by Dev 1, 17 Nov. For an annual-plan target with a project center, the project center wins and no disagreement is checked, as on `main`.
2. **`placeColumnsForTargets()`** is added next to `placeForTarget()`. A `ReportPlace` cannot say "shared" (D-90), but the writers need `all_centers`. Both follow one rule; `placeForTarget()` reads `placeColumnsForTargets()`. Not in `contracts.md`: Dev 1 to ratify.
3. **Proposal targets in two edge cases:** the target gets city and program, the center stays null, and the dry run lists it. That is what the backfill and the writers do when a project's center and its row disagree (`disagree_ids`), and when a proposal target's project matches no row of its proposal (`no_row_ids`). Neither case exists on local data.
