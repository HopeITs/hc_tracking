## The date column for D-94

**There is none, so D-94 resolves to `created_at` for every risk.** `risks` has `id, impact, name, name_en, project_id, proposal_location_id, library_risk_id, result_id, proposal_id, happened, likelihood, severity, solution, created_at, updated_at`; none of the four risk migrations (`create_risks_table`, `add_likelihood_and_severity_to_risks_table`, `create_risk_library_table`, `add_library_columns_to_risks_table`) ever added an "identified" or "raised" date. D-94 says "the day it was identified, **else the day it was recorded**", and the recorded day is `created_at`. A risk with a null `created_at` falls to the row of the most recent proposal, like any uncovered date.

## Deviations from contracts.md / the prompt

1. **`ProjectOwnedRecipients` is not renamed away**, for the reason above. Confirmed by Dev 1 (23 Nov); Y-16 should delete it when indicators and templates move.
2. **`riskAwareness()`'s groups are told apart by the row, not by `proposal_id`**, and `resources/views/pages/projects/_show-risk-awareness.blade.php` now groups by `Risk::isOnLogframe()`. The prompt puts that panel in slice 2, but the backfill gives every register risk a `proposal_id`, so without this one-line change the register group would have silently moved under a proposal's heading the moment the backfill ran. Slice 2 still owns the panel's design.
3. **`saveRisk()` on the register writes the row** (`ProjectSuccessor::rowFor($projectId, today)`) and authorizes `create` against it, while the form still picks a project. Agreed with the developer at the keyboard: otherwise every risk logged between the two slices would have no row and would drop out of the digest and the strategy figures. Slice 2 replaces the picker with `riskLocationId`.
4. **The register's search matches the place through HR ids, not a join.** A place's city and program
   names live in the HR database, so each search word is resolved to HR ids first
   (`searchedPlaceIds()`, the pattern `FiltersProjectDirectory` established) and the register's own
   query only does `whereIn('city_id', …)` / `whereIn('program_id', …)`. `AGENT.md §5` forbids the join.
5. **The card keeps linking to the project page** (`route('projects.show', $risk->project_id)`) because
   a row has no page of its own until Y-16. A risk the backfill could not map shows its project's name
   as plain text instead of a place.
6. **Three risk-carrying test files moved their risks onto rows but keep `Project::factory()` for their other subjects** (`WeeklyDigestTest` tasks, `StrategyRollupTest` plan indicators and reports, `MonitoringActivityTest` indicators). `wave4/AGENT.md §8.6` asks a touched test file to stop building projects; those subjects have no row column before Y-16, so it is not yet possible. No test was deleted.
