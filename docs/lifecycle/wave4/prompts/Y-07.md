## Rules
- A template's questions are independent of the bank after the copy: editing one never writes `narrative_questions`, and a later change to the bank never rewrites a template.
- `text_ar` is required; `text_en` optional; `frequency` is `monthly` or `quarter_end` and is fixed after creation when the question has answers (changing when it is asked would orphan them).
- Order is per frequency, exactly as on the bank's page. A move is a move in the whole frequency.
- Switched-off questions are not asked in any month from the current one on. Draft reports stop showing them; an answer already typed in a draft is **kept in the table and not shown**, so switching back on restores it.
- A new or re-activated question appears in draft reports at once, and in the right months (`quarter_end` → Mar, Jun, Sep, Dec).
- Stale check: `expectedUpdatedAt` on save; a mismatch writes nothing and returns the page's standard stale message.
- Record each change on the template (`RecordsActivity`): `report_template.question_added`, `.question_changed`, `.question_switched`, `.question_removed`, with the question id and no text of an answer.
- **Slice 2:** the questions panel on the template tab becomes editable for a manager, and stays the read-only list for a viewer. Mirror the bank's page: rows grouped under **Monthly** and **Quarter end**, one status word each (On / Switched off), Edit as the row's main action, Switch off / Switch on and Delete in the ⋯ menu, up / down arrows with the refocus-after-move script. English wording "Switch off" / "Switched off", Arabic إيقاف / متوقف / تشغيل, as the bank's rule says.
- A question that came from the bank shows a small "From the bank" label; an own question shows "This report only".
- The delete dialog on a question with answers offers "Switch off instead".
- When the template covers two or more proposals, the panel says once, above the list: "These questions are asked in the one report that covers: {proposals}".
