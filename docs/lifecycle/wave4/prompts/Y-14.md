## Rules
- **Migrations** (`AGENT.md §4`): add `program_id`, `center_id`, `city_wide`, `scope_key` → backfill → second migration: unique on `scope_key`, drop `meal_coverages_project_id_unique`, make `project_id` nullable (it already is) and stop writing it.
