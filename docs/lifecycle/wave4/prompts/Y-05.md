## Rules
- **One query, three readers.** The matrix, the export and the list call `ReportExtraction`; none builds its own `where`. A figure can then never differ between screen and file.
- **Filters on the page:** Proposal · City · Program · Center · From · To stay visible; donor, indicator and pillar stay behind **More filters** as today (`.ai/rules/reports.md`). City narrows Center; Program narrows Center; a Center pre-selects its City.
- **Center options** are HR centers that hold at least one template, labelled "center · city". A city-wide place (D-88) is its own option, **"Field work · city"**, one per city that has one: it is an answer, not a gap. Places whose center is not set appear once as **"Center not set"**, so nothing is hidden while the team is still entering centers. The two are never merged.
- **Shared targets (D-90).** In the center view a shared target shows each center's achieved figure on its own row and the target value marked "shared with N centers"; every total and every export row counts that target value **once**, with the centers' achieved figures summed (counts) or pooled (ratios). Filtering to one of the centers shows that center's achieved figure against the whole shared target, labelled as shared, never a divided target.
- **From / To** are month pickers. They replace the year select as the window; a "This year" shortcut sets Jan → this month. `From > To` is refused with a field error, not silently swapped. While exactly one proposal is filtered, a "Whole grant" shortcut sets its actual period.
- The old `?year=` and `?center=<project id>` URLs still open: translate them once on mount to `from`/`to` and to the project's place, then rewrite the URL.
- Choosing the view is unchanged: a donor or proposal filter selects the donor view, otherwise the center view.
- Only validated figures are summed; pending ones are shown, never added (§14). Ratios only through `pooledRatioSql()`.
- **Export:** the file carries a header block stating every active filter in words and the window, then the same rows and totals as the screen, in the page's language. No money. Payroll proposals excluded.
- **Slice 2:** `monthly-reports` gains Proposal, Program and From / To on both tabs. The big figures and their counts read every filter except the pressed figure itself, so a click shows exactly that many (the rule the reports register already follows).
- A matrix cell links to `monthly-reports` with the same filters and that month; a report row links back to the matrix for its place.
- Performance: no query per row or per month. Assert the query count of the matrix with 3 places × 12 months × 5 indicators.
- D-74: `ReportExtraction` and `ExtractionFilter` join the guard test; they never name `Project`.
