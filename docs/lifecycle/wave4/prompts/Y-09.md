## Goal
An approved proposal can still change, but only as a **change request** (D-89): the PR team opens it on the proposal, says what changes and why; the **Finance Manager** and the **MEAL Manager** review it; then the **PR Manager** approves or rejects it (D-89a); only an approved request changes the proposal. Each request is kept with who asked, who reviewed, who decided, when, the reason and the before / after values. Today `SaveProposal` edits an approved proposal directly and silently (only a beneficiaries change is logged), and after Y-01 a center cannot be changed or removed at all once the proposal is approved.

- **Slice 1 (Dev 1):** the `proposal_change_requests` table, the actions (request, review, approve, reject, withdraw), the lock on direct post-approval edits, the guard on centers that already report, events and notifications. **[contract]**
- **Slice 2 (Dev 2):** the "Request a change" flow, the reviewers' screen, the PR Manager's decision screen, and the "Change requests" chapter on the proposal page.

Last in the wave. It does not block Y-02…Y-08: until it ships, a center entered by mistake stays as entered.

A decided row is never edited or deleted.
> - **`proposal_change_request_reviews`** (Y-09, D-89a): `proposal_change_request_id` FK cascade, `slot` (`finance_manager` | `meal_manager`), `verdict` (`ChangeReviewVerdict`: `no_objection`, `remarks`, `skipped`) null while waiting, `note` text null (required with `remarks`), `reviewed_by` FK users null nullOnDelete, `reviewed_at` datetime null, timestamps. Unique (`proposal_change_request_id`, `slot`). Both rows are created with the request.

In `§6`, change the refusal sentence of the "Post-approval center fill" paragraph to, and add after it:

> Changing or removing a center, or changing a row's city, program or responsible, is refused **outside an approved change request** (`proposals.location_locked_after_approval`).
>
> **Change request (Y-09, D-89, D-89a).** On an approved proposal `SaveProposal` called directly accepts only the center fill (D-83); any other difference is refused (`proposals.change_request_required`).
> - `RequestProposalChange::handle(Proposal $proposal, array $data, string $reason, ?string $expectedUpdatedAt = null): ProposalChangeRequest`. Authorizes `ProposalPolicy::requestChange` (`proposals.update` AND status `isApprovedByDonor()`). `$reason` required, ≥ 10 characters. `$data` takes the keys `SaveProposal` accepts on an approved proposal, plus `locations[]` without the fill-only limit. Validates as `SaveProposal` would, computes the differences, refuses an empty one (`proposals.change_request_empty`), stores a `pending` row with its two review rows and **changes nothing on the proposal**. A slot with no active holder gets its review row as `skipped` at once. Records `proposal.change_requested`, dispatches `ProposalChangeRequested`.
> - `ReviewProposalChange::handle(ProposalChangeRequest $request, string $slot, string $verdict, ?string $note = null): ProposalChangeRequestReview`. Authorizes `ProposalPolicy::reviewChange`: the user's employee is the active holder of `$slot` (`Slots`). Only on a `pending` request; `verdict` is `no_objection` or `remarks`; `remarks` needs a note ≥ 10. A reviewer may change their review until the request is decided. Records `proposal.change_reviewed`; when the last waiting review comes in, dispatches `ProposalChangeReviewed`.
> - `ApproveProposalChange::handle(ProposalChangeRequest $request, string $expectedStatus = 'pending'): ProposalChangeRequest`. Authorizes `ProposalPolicy::decideChange` (`proposals.approve`). Refused while a review is still waiting (`proposals.change_request_reviews_pending`); a `remarks` verdict does not block. Inside one transaction on the locked proposal: re-validates the payload against the proposal as it is **now**, refuses when a stored `from` no longer matches (`proposals.change_request_stale`), applies it through `SaveProposal`, marks the row `approved`. Records `proposal.change_approved`, dispatches `ProposalChangeDecided`.
> - `RejectProposalChange::handle(ProposalChangeRequest $request, string $note, string $expectedStatus = 'pending'): ProposalChangeRequest`. Same policy. `$note` required, ≥ 10. Marks the row `rejected`; the proposal is untouched. Records `proposal.change_rejected`, dispatches `ProposalChangeDecided`.
> - A requester who holds `proposals.approve` may decide their own request (D-89a). The requester may withdraw a `pending` request: `WithdrawProposalChange` deletes nothing and marks it `rejected` with the note "withdrawn by the requester".
>
> **Centers that already report.** A change that moves or removes a center (or turns a row city-wide or back) is refused (`proposals.location_center_reports`) when a report template of this proposal at that place has a monthly report that is `submitted` or `validated`; checked when the request is opened **and** when it is approved. Otherwise the place's template, its lines and its targets are **not** moved by the approval: the change is applied to the row and the affected templates are listed in the notification for MEAL to settle.

## Rules
- **One door.** After this task there are exactly three ways an approved proposal changes: the center fill (Y-01, no reason, no approval), `ExtendProposal` (D-60, its own reason), and an approved change request. Assert it: a direct `SaveProposal` edit of an approved proposal with any other difference is refused.
- **No new permission.** Request = `proposals.update`; review = holding the slot; decide = `proposals.approve` (D-70). Do not touch `config/access-control.php`. The General Manager does not hold `proposals.approve` and therefore cannot decide: that is intended.
- **Review is advice, not a second approval.** Both reviews must be in before Approve; neither reviewer can approve, reject or apply. A reviewer who is also the requester still reviews in their slot. Amounts in the from → to list follow the usual money rule for each reader.
- **Never a request nobody can move.** If a slot holder changes while a review is waiting, the new holder reviews. If a slot becomes empty, its waiting review is marked `skipped` when the decision screen is next opened (checked inside `ApproveProposalChange` as well).
- The existing `proposal.beneficiaries_changed` activity stays (other readers use it); it is now written when a change request is approved, with the same payload.
- `changes` and `payload` store ids and plain values, never names from HR: names are resolved at read time (`HrCenter` / `HrCity::remembered()`). No money field is written to an activity; amounts inside `changes` are shown only to money holders.
- Approval does **not** re-run donor approval, `MaterialiseProposalLocations`, pre-fill or placement. A center **added** to a row creates no project and no template here: say so on the decision screen, and list "new place without a template" in the handoff as the follow-up for Dev 1.
- While a request is `pending` the proposal shows a "Change waiting for approval" notice; the center fill and `ExtendProposal` stay available; a second request is refused until the first is decided.
- D-74: no read of `projects` in new code. The "already reports" guard reads templates through `ReportTemplate::query()->atPlace()` and the proposal pivot.
- **Slice 2, requester:** one button on the proposal page, **Request a change**, shown only on an approved or active proposal to holders of `proposals.update`. It opens the proposal form with a required **Reason** field at the top and a summary step before sending: "You are asking to change: …" (field, from → to). The ordinary Edit button is hidden on an approved proposal; "Add centers" (Y-01) stays.
- **Slice 2, reviewers:** a status button "Changes to review" on the `proposals` register for the two slot holders (requests whose review in their slot is waiting). The review screen shows the reason and the from → to list, with **No objection** and **I have remarks** (asks a note).
- **Slice 2, PR Manager:** a status button "Changes to approve" on the `proposals` register (`setFocus()`, like the other registers; count equals the list; a request still waiting for a review is listed with the words "Waiting for Finance" / "Waiting for MEAL"). The decision screen shows the reason, the from → to list and both reviews with their notes, with **Approve** (disabled, with its reason in words, while a review is waiting) and **Reject** (asks a note). A stale request says which field changed meanwhile and offers only Reject.
- **Change requests chapter** on the proposal page: newest first, each with its status word (Waiting / Approved / Rejected, never colour alone), the requester, the reason, the from → to list, the two reviews (who, verdict, note, or "no holder") and the decision (who, when, note). Empty state says no change has been requested.
- Words: "Change request" / «طلب تغيير» everywhere; never "edit" for an approved proposal.
