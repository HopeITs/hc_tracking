# Y-19 · A location row is deactivated, never deleted; its unreached share moves to other rows · Dev 1 (slices 1, 4) + Dev 2 (slices 2, 3) · L (4–5 days) · 4 slices · needs Y-09, Y-10, Y-11 slice 1, D-96

## Goal
A proposal's target is a **total** per indicator, and each location row carries a **share** of it (D-96, revising D-91). Centers close and new ones join during a proposal. When that happens, nothing is deleted and no figures are mixed. Two scenarios drive this task:

- **Replacement.** Row "Homs · Education · Al-Waer" has a share of 100 pupils. It closes on 1 March with 30 reached. A new row for Al-Inshaat joins from 5 March and replaces it. Al-Waer keeps its 30 under its own name and stops at 1 March. Al-Inshaat is offered the remaining 70. The total stays 100.
- **No replacement.** 1,000 pupils across five rows (different cities, one program), roughly 200 each but not equal, because capacity differs. Aleppo closes with 50 of 200 reached. The system **proposes** how to spread the remaining 150 over the four active rows. MEAL edits the split and saves it. The total stays 1,000.

- **Slice 1 (Dev 1):** schema, actions and contract: deactivating a row or a center, the "replaces" link, moving a remainder, and achievement counted to the deactivation date. **[contract]**
- **Slice 2 (Dev 2):** the proposal form and change request: "Deactivate" replaces "Remove" for a row that can no longer be deleted; a new row can name the row it replaces.
- **Slice 3 (Dev 2):** the targets grid: an inactive column, and the "move the remainder" dialog with the proposed split, which MEAL edits.
- **Slice 4 (Dev 1):** report template lines follow the centers of a row after approval. A center that starts reporting a target (it joins a row, so the target turns shared under D-90, or it sits on a replacement row) gets a pending line in its template; a center that leaves or a row that is deactivated has its lines retired from that month. Closes the template-line gap named in the Y-10 handoff (assigned here by Dev 1, 23 Nov).

## Answered by Dev 1 (23 Nov)
1. **Draft proposals.** A row of a proposal that was **never approved** can still be removed, using the 19 Nov dialog. From approval on, a row can only be deactivated.
2. **Approved proposals.** Deactivating a row or a center, and adding a replacement row, on an approved proposal is a **change request** (Y-09, D-89a) and applies only when it is approved. Moving the remainder is MEAL's own act afterwards (`targets.update`), with no change request.
3. **Proposed split.** In proportion to each active row's current share, rounded to whole units, with the rounding difference given to the largest share. With a replacement row, all of the remainder goes to that row.
4. **Unlink from the project page.** It becomes "Deactivate" (see Rules), built in slice 2.

## [contract] (D-96)
Add to `contracts.md §7`:

> - **Deactivated row (Y-19, D-96):** `proposal_locations.deactivated_on` date null, `deactivation_reason` varchar(500) null, `replaces_location_id` FK `proposal_locations` null nullOnDelete, indexed (`proposal_locations_replaces_location_id_foreign`). A row with `deactivated_on` set is **inactive** from that day: it stays on the proposal with its targets, reports and figures, and is never deleted once its proposal has been approved. A row joining later has its own `start_date` (Y-11), which is its joining date. `replaces_location_id` must name an inactive row of the same proposal and program. `proposal_location_centers.left_on` date null: a center leaving a multi-center row (D-90) on that date; the row stays active.
> - The unique index `proposal_locations_proposal_city_program_unique` is replaced by `proposal_locations_active_place_unique` on (`proposal_id`, `city_id`, `program_id`, `active_key`), where `active_key` is a stored generated column `IF(deactivated_on IS NULL, 1, NULL)`. This allows one **active** row per place and any number of inactive ones.
> - Actions: `DeactivateLocation::handle(Proposal $proposal, int $locationId, CarbonImmutable $on, string $reason, ?int $replacedById = null): ProposalLocation` and `ReleaseLocationCenter::handle(Proposal $proposal, int $locationId, int $centerId, CarbonImmutable $on): void` (`proposals.update`; on an approved proposal, only from an approved change request).

Add to `contracts.md §12.1`:

> - **Share of a total (Y-19, D-96, revises D-91):** a proposal target of a row is that row's **share**. The proposal's total for an indicator is the sum of its rows' shares, active and inactive. Deactivating a row freezes its share at its achieved value on `deactivated_on` (`achievedValue($deactivatedOn)`); the difference is its **remainder**. `ReallocateTargetRemainder::handle(Proposal $proposal, int $fromLocationId, int $indicatorId, array $shares): void` (`targets.update`, MEAL) moves a remainder to active rows of the same proposal. `$shares` is `{location_id, amount}` and must add up exactly to the remainder. It writes one `target_reallocations` row per receiving row (`plan_indicator_from_id`, `plan_indicator_to_id`, `amount` decimal(12,2), `original_value` decimal(12,2), `user_id`, `created_at`), so a share's history is never lost. The total never changes.
> - Achievement of an inactive row's target counts only reports dated on or before `deactivated_on`. Reports already submitted stay as they are.
> - Only **summed count** targets are reallocated. Percentage, average and level targets (`measuresLevel()`) are frozen with their row and nothing is moved.

## Rules
- **Slice 1, migrations** (`wave4/AGENT.md §4`, additive first): add the columns → add `active_key` and the new unique → drop the old unique → create `target_reallocations`. Each `down()` works on data. Dropping the old unique must restore any FK index MySQL needs; Y-10's second migration shows the trap.
- `DeactivateLocation` runs in one transaction. It sets the date and reason, freezes each summed-count target of the row (`value` := achieved on the date, `original_value` recorded in a `target_reallocations` row with `plan_indicator_to_id` null), and records one `proposal.location_deactivated` activity with counts only. It does **not** move the remainder: that is MEAL's step.
- A replacement row created with `replaces_location_id` gets, per indicator of the replaced row, a **proposed** share equal to the remainder. The proposal is shown in the grid (slice 3); nothing is saved until MEAL saves it.
- `ProposalLocationRows::replace()` from Y-10 refuses to drop a row with targets. On an approved proposal it now refuses every drop with `proposals.location_deactivate_instead`. The UI never offers removal there.
- `ClearLocationTargets` and the 19 Nov dialog stay for **never-approved** proposals only (answer 1).
- **Unlink from the project page** (`ReadsProjectFunding::detachProposal`, answer 4): on an approved proposal, unlinking a project whose row has targets no longer shows a refusal toast. It opens the same "Deactivate" dialog (date, reason, optional replacement), which goes through the change request like the form does.
- Achievement: `achievedValue()` and `achievedValueSql()` take the row's `deactivated_on` as the upper bound when no tighter `$through` is given, and must stay in step (one test runs both).
- `SaveProposalTargetsGrid` refuses a new or changed value on an inactive row with `targets.cell_row_inactive`, the same way the waiting row is refused in Y-10. Clearing is also refused: an inactive row's share is history.
- A center that left a row (`left_on`) is not offered in that row's report months after `left_on`. The row's shared target (D-90) is unchanged: the remaining centers carry it.
- **Slice 2 (form, change request):** an active row of an approved proposal shows "Deactivate" (date, reason, optional "replaced by a new row"). A new row can pick "Replaces…" from the inactive rows of the same program. Both go through the Y-09 change request on an approved proposal. Inactive rows are listed under the active ones, greyed, with "Inactive since {date}" and their reason.
- **Slice 3 (grid):** an inactive row stays a column, read-only, headed "{city · program} — inactive since {date}". Each cell shows "{achieved} of {original}". A row with an unmoved remainder shows "{remainder} not moved yet" and a **"Move the remainder"** button for `targets.update` holders. The dialog lists the active rows with their current share and the proposed amount in an editable field. It shows the running sum against the remainder, and Save is disabled until they match. Wording is plain, in en and ar (Arabic plurals in explicit ranges).
- **Slice 4 (template lines):** one action, `SyncRowTemplateLines::handle(Proposal $proposal, int $locationId): void`, called after commit by every writer that changes a row's centers or its active state (`LinkProposalProject` / `attachProject()`, `DeactivateLocation`, `ReleaseLocationCenter`, an approved change request adding a row).
  - A center with no line for a row target gets one in its template at that place, as a **pending** line through the Y-04 path: the joined template if the proposal has a placement there, else the D-77 choice (a `pending` placement for MEAL). It never creates a template silently.
  - An existing line is never duplicated (D-86 overlapping lines: one line, one more share).
  - A departed center or a deactivated row retires its lines with `RetireReportTemplateLine` from the first month after `left_on` / `deactivated_on`. Submitted or validated reports are untouched.
  - Idempotent: running it twice changes nothing. `report-templates:prefill-active` gives the same result for existing data.
  - Confirming the new line reaches draft reports only through `RefreshMonthlyReportLines` (D-41).
- The grid must not scroll the page sideways at 375 px (as in Y-10).
- D-74: no new read of `projects`.

## Stop and escalate to Dev 1
- A remainder on a percentage, average or level target that someone wants moved.
- A wish to change the proposal's total because a row closed.
- A deactivation dated before a report that is already submitted or validated for that row.
- A replacement row in another program.
- Donor reports (`DonorReport`) showing a different total after a reallocation.

## Do not
Delete a row of an approved proposal or any of its targets or reports. Change annual-plan targets (D-44). Split a shared row target between its centers (D-90). Drop or stop writing `plan_indicators.project_id` (Y-18 does that). Change monthly report or template code beyond slice 4 (add and retire lines through the existing Y-04 actions) and hiding a departed center's months. Create a template without MEAL's D-77 choice.
