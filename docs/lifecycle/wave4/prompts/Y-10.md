## Goal
MEAL enters a proposal's targets **per location row** (city × program), not per center (D-91). A row with several centers gets one target that its centers reach together (D-90); a row with one center or a field-work row gets its target at that one place. Today the grid has one column per linked **project**, and after Y-01 an approval creates one project per center, so a row with two centers would show two columns and ask for two targets: the opposite of the ruling.

- **Slice 1 (Dev 1):** the target is keyed by its location row; `SaveProposalTargetsGrid` takes rows; backfill. **[contract]**
- **Slice 2 (Dev 2):** the grid's columns are the proposal's location rows.

Runs right after Y-08 and before Y-03, alone: it changes `plan_indicators`.

## Rules
- **Slice 1, migrations** (`wave4/AGENT.md §4`, additive first): add `proposal_location_id` nullable → backfill each `proposal` target from the row of its proposal that carries its `project_id`, else the row with the same city and program → second migration: the unique index.
- **Two targets on one row.** If the backfill finds two targets of one indicator on one row, it writes neither link, lists both and stops before the unique index: merging targets is a human decision (D-80). Command `targets:row-backfill {--dry-run}` prints: targets, linked, unlinked (ids), rows with a duplicate (ids).
- When a row's centers change (the center fill of Y-01, an approved change request of Y-09), the place of its targets is rewritten in the **same transaction**: one method, `PlanIndicator::syncPlaceFromRow()`, called from `ProposalLocationRows`. A target can therefore turn from single-center to shared when a second center is added. Its existing template lines are not touched here: list it in the handoff.
- `PlaceBridge::placeForTarget()` (Y-08) now reads the row; drop its `projectId` branch for proposal targets and say so in its PHPDoc.
- `ManagesTargetForm::saveTarget`: a proposal target picks a **location row**, not a project. Annual-plan targets are unchanged.
- D-23, D-25, D-50a and the concurrency rule of the grid are unchanged: same error keys, with `cells.{i}` now naming a row.
- D-74: no new read of `projects`. `project_id` is written from the row only.
- **Slice 2:** one column per location row, headed "city · program", with the row's centers under it as chips ("Field work" for a field-work row, "Center not set" for a row with none). A row with several centers says, once, under its header: "One target for these centers together". No per-center column, no way to split a target.
- A row that is waiting for its program (`program_requests` pending) shows its column disabled with the reason in words.
- The grid must not scroll the page horizontally at 375 px: the first column stays in place and the rows scroll inside the grid, as today.
