## Goal
One monthly report per **template** per month (D-76), instead of one per project. A center with one template enters one report; a center with two templates enters two. Every monthly-report action, the generator and the pages read the template. The figures, the workflow, the deadlines and the mirrors in `reports` do not change.

- **Slice 1:** schema, backfill with its dry run, model relation, generator and `monthly-reports:prepare` / `:backfill`.
- **Slice 2:** line builder, periods, the fourteen actions, policy and the "may validate" scope.
- **Slice 3:** pages (`monthly-reports/index`, `show`), notifications and alerts, the desk matrix card.

## Rules
- **Slice 1, migrations** (`wave4/AGENT.md §4`): add `report_template_id` nullable → backfill each report from its project's template (`report_templates.project_id`, the one legacy read, inside the migration) → second migration: not null, new unique (`report_template_id`, `period`), drop unique (`project_id`, `period`), `project_id` nullable. A report whose project has **no template** is listed by the dry run and stops the migration before DDL.
- Command `monthly-reports:template-backfill {--dry-run}`: reports, mapped, unmapped (must be 0), by status.
- Slugs do not change. Existing URLs keep working.
- `MonthlyReport::template(): BelongsTo`. `project()` stays for old readers and is marked `@deprecated Y-03`.
- **Legacy mirror:** while `project_activity_statuses` and other modules read `monthly_reports.project_id`, new reports keep filling it through `PlaceBridge::legacyProjectIdFor($template)`. When two templates share a place it is the same project for both; that is expected and is why the column is no longer unique.
- **Slice 2:** `MonthlyReportLineBuilder::templateLines()`, `missingLineCount()`, `attributionOf()` and `MonthlyReportPeriods::for()` / `fromLoaded()` take a `ReportTemplate`. `attributionOf` reads the template's `program_id` (`programs.donor_attribution`), not a project.
- Sequencing (D-08) is **per template**: an unsubmitted month of template A never blocks template B at the same place.
- Signatures frozen in `handoffs/T17A.md` keep their names and their `expectedStatus` parameter. Only `OpenMonthlyReport` changes its first argument (`Project` → `ReportTemplate`); list it under Deviations with Dev 1's approval.
- `MonthlyReportPolicy`: `open` takes the template; the preparer rule is the `[contract]` item 9 text, word for word: any responsible of a location row of any of the template's proposals at its place, or a supervisor of one. A responsible of a row at another place, or of a proposal not on the template, gets nothing from this rule. validate / return / decideReopen keep their rule and reach coverage through `PlaceBridge` (item 9, last sentence).
- The validate scope ("reports this user may validate") keeps its three cases; assert the count is unchanged on migrated data.
- **Slice 3:** a report's header names its **place** (city · program · center) and its **proposals**. When the manager has two reports for the same place and month, each row says which proposals it covers, so they cannot be confused. Notifications name the proposals the same way.
- Extend `WaveFourProjectsGuardTest` with every class this task touches under `app/Actions/MonthlyReports/**` and `app/Support/MonthlyReport*.php`.
