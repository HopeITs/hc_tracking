## Rules
- **Migrations** (`AGENT.md §4`): add `proposal_location_id` nullable → backfill. No tightening.
- **Backfill (D-94):** a risk with a `project_id` goes to `ProjectSuccessor::rowFor($projectId, its identification date ?? created_at)`; `proposal_id` is filled from the row when null. A risk that already has a `proposal_id` **and** a project goes to the row of that proposal carrying the project, with no date rule. Unmapped risks are listed and left.
- Command `risks:row-backfill {--dry-run}`: risks, moved per row, by date / by "most recent" / by their own proposal, unmapped (ids).
- `Risk::location(): BelongsTo`; `activityProgramId()` and the delete snapshot read `ProposalLocation::programIdFor` / `ownerEmployeeIdFor`.
- `RiskPolicy::create(User, ?int $locationId)` and `update`: `risks.*` or the row in `ownedLocationIds()`. `attach` / `detach` unchanged.
- `ProjectOwnedRecipients` becomes `LocationOwnedRecipients` (the subject's `proposal_location_id` → the row's owner); `RiskRecipients` extends it. Any other subclass keeps working: list them in the plan and move each.
- `WeeklyDigestScanner` and `StrategyRollup` join `risks.proposal_location_id` to `proposal_locations`; their figures must not change on data where each project has one row (assert it).
- `ReadsProjectDelivery::riskAwareness()` keeps its two groups (by proposal, and the place's own register) but reads the row.
- **Slice 2:** `risks/⚡index`: the `project` URL filter becomes `location`; the create form picks a row (`riskLocationId`); `projectRiskChart()` becomes a per-row chart (top 8), fed by the same `filteredQuery()`; search still matches the place's name. Keep the three lang keys named in `risks.md` in use.
- The user-facing word stays "Project" (`AGENT.md §8`).
