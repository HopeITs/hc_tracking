## [contract]
Slice 1 · in `contracts.md §11.2`, the `activity_schedules` bullet (D-49):

> `proposal_location_id` FK `proposal_locations` null cascadeOnDelete replaces `project_id` as the override key: rows with a null location are the proposal's calendar; rows for a location, when any exist for that activity, replace it for that location. Unique (`result_id`, `proposal_location_id`, `month`). `ActivitySchedule::effectiveFor(int $activityId, ?int $locationId): array` is the only implementation of that rule. `SaveActivitySchedule::handle(Proposal $proposal, int $activityId, ?int $locationId, array $input): array`. `project_id` is kept and still written until Y-18.

Slice 2 · in `§13`, the `project_activity_statuses` bullet (D-51):

A report's activities are those of its template's proposals; each is written on the row of **its own proposal** at the template's place. `project_id` is kept and still written until Y-18.
