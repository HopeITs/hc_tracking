## What "open" means
| Type | Open | Who sees it |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly report | `draft` (only once its month has started) or `submitted` | the templates the viewer prepares (`MonthlyReportPreparers::templateIdsFor`) plus submitted reports they validate (`MonthlyReport::validatableBy`) |
| Donor report | `submitted_on` is null (Due, Overdue) | as `DonorReportPolicy::view`: `funding-agreements.view` or `reports.aggregate`, else the responsible employee |
| Existing report | `draft`, `reviewed`, and not a mirror of a monthly report | `reports.view` |

"Needs me": monthly = own draft of a template they prepare, or a submitted report they validate; donor = they are the responsible; existing = own draft, or a reviewed report of someone else when they hold `reports.approve`.
No permission was added or changed. No amount is read (a test asserts no money table or column in the SQL).
