## Panels: fixed height, fitted pages, drafts until Done (user decisions 2026-10-28)
Every panel is one fixed height (desk.css --panel-h) and its body never spills (no height:auto / overflow:visible overrides). Rows per page are not a constant: a list marked with `$this->fitAttributes($pg)` is measured in the browser (desk-filters.js) and asks for as many rows as fit (`fitRows`); a full page shares the leftover height between its rows (.dk-fit-fill). Wide cards put a row on one line via `@container pb` queries, not `[style*="--span:N"]` selectors (spans change under the 1280px media query). Panel filters and the Customize sheet edit a browser-side draft; nothing reaches Livewire until Done (`applyFilters($draft)` writes only facets and the panel's `filterProperties()`; `saveLayout($layout)`). A panel with nothing to filter shows no funnel. The Annual plans panel was removed. Tasks filter by the four phases only (no "Open"). Monthly reports: no legend, the status filter lives in the popup. Report templates "Create" pre-fills the template (ReportTemplatePrefiller) and opens the project's `?tab=template`. The panel body is `overflow: clip`, never `hidden` + `overscroll-behavior: contain`: a hidden box is a scroll container and swallowed the wheel, so the page would not scroll over a panel. At a glance is 3 columns × 2 rows from M to W, one row only at XL. My team has a header search button (x-desk.panel `search=`), which filters live as you type. Notification preferences and rules are reached from the bell's panel, not the profile menu.
