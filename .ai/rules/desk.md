## Panels: fixed height, fitted pages, drafts until Done (user decisions 2026-10-28)
Every panel is one fixed height (desk.css --panel-h) and its body never spills (no height:auto / overflow:visible overrides). Rows per page are not a constant: a list marked with `$this->fitAttributes($pg)` is measured in the browser (desk-filters.js) and asks for as many rows as fit (`fitRows`); a full page shares the leftover height between its rows (.dk-fit-fill). Wide cards put a row on one line via `@container pb` queries, not `[style*="--span:N"]` selectors (spans change under the 1280px media query). Panel filters and the Customize sheet edit a browser-side draft; nothing reaches Livewire until Done (`applyFilters($draft)` writes only facets and the panel's `filterProperties()`; `saveLayout($layout)`). A panel with nothing to filter shows no funnel. The Annual plans panel was removed. Tasks filter by the four phases only (no "Open"). Monthly reports: no legend, the status filter lives in the popup. Report templates "Create" pre-fills the template (ReportTemplatePrefiller) and opens the project's `?tab=template`. The panel body is `overflow: clip`, never `hidden` + `overscroll-behavior: contain`: a hidden box is a scroll container and swallowed the wheel, so the page would not scroll over a panel. At a glance is 3 columns × 2 rows from M to W, one row only at XL. My team has a header search button (x-desk.panel `search=`), which filters live as you type. Notification preferences and rules are reached from the bell's panel, not the profile menu.

## Every page and action can be on the Desk (user decision 2026-11-09)
A Desk item is one of: a hand-made panel (`App\Support\Desk\Panels::definitions`, own component), a page card `p-<slug>` (`App\Support\Desk\PageCards`, one renderer `desk.panels.page`, data from `App\Support\Desk\Cards\*`), or a button `a-<action>` / `g-<slug>` (`App\Support\Desk\Shortcuts`, drawn by `x-desk.tile` in the Desk page itself, no component of its own). `<slug>` is `Destinations::slugOf($route)` (dots to dashes, `.index` dropped), so ids stay selector-safe. Cards and buttons are offered exactly when `Destinations::allows()` allows their page: never add a second permission rule for them. `Panels::itemForRoute()` decides what a page becomes (its panel, else its card, else a page button); the sidebar drag-and-drop, the sidebar "+", every register's "Add to desk" (`x-desk.add-to-desk`, inside `x-desk.register`) and the Ask bar all go through it. Buttons may be one row unit tall (`Panels::MinTileRows`, `desk-rows.js minRowsOf`, both keyed on the `a-`/`g-` prefix); everything else stays at least `MinRows`, and a row is never shorter than its tallest minimum. A card reads its rows with the page's own rules (e.g. DonorReportsCard repeats DonorReportPolicy::view) and eager-loads every relation it prints: `Model::shouldBeStrict` is on in tests.

## Desk tabs: pages opened from My desk load in tabs, never replace it (user decision 2026-11-09)
On `dashboard` only, `resources/js/desk-tabs.js` turns every same-origin page link (panels, buttons, sidebar, notifications, Ask rows) into a tab: an iframe of the real page with `?_tab=1`. `App\Support\Desk\DeskTab::current()` (that param, or `Sec-Fetch-Dest: iframe`) makes `layouts/app/sidebar` drop the side-nav, top bar, Ask, tour, preloader and notification toasts, and leave out `data-user-id`, so a tab never opens a second Echo subscription (see js.md). `desk-tab-child.js` keeps `_tab=1` on in-tab navigation, sends links to the Desk back to the Desk tab, and reports title/URL to the parent by postMessage (same origin only). Links are taken on `mousedown` (capture) before Livewire's own `wire:navigate` press handler, so no page is prefetched and thrown away; `livewire:navigate` is the fallback. A link that must leave the Desk carries `data-desk-native`; downloads, exports, prints and previews are never tabbed (`NotForTabs`). The tab host is `wire:ignore`: the Desk re-renders often and must never reload a frame. Tabs live in localStorage per user; the "Open pages in desk tabs" switch in the avatar menu turns the feature off per browser.

## The Ask bar answers in two layers (2026-11-09)
`App\Support\Desk\AskCommands` builds a permission-filtered index (pages, "New …" actions, what stands for each page on the Desk, ready layouts, verbs) from `AskEngine`'s own vocabulary; `resources/js/ask-index.js` searches it on every key (normalisation, typo allowance and wrong-keyboard-layout reading mirror `AskEngine` / `KeyboardLayout`). The index is rendered once per page by `x-desk.ask-index` in the layout, outside the Livewire `desk.ask` component: inside it, every debounced search round trip would send it again. The server still answers questions and finds records; while instant rows show, its "Go to" and "Do" groups stay in the markup (tests read them) but are hidden, so nothing shows twice. One highlight runs across both layers and is drawn by `desk-ask.js` (`sync()`), not by `x-bind` on server rows.

## Desk cards are solid and still; lens rows never overflow
No backdrop-filter on Desk cards and no animated background behind them (2026-11-09): blurred glass over a moving field re-blurred every card each frame and made scroll/drag/resize lag. A panel's tools row (`.dk-ptools`) is one line: `.dk-seg` is `display: contents` (desk-shell.css), so its buttons are the row's flex items; under a 340px card they tighten and elide (desk-grid.css), and Needs you's Focus/List show their icons (`deck`, `list`) alone. A 3-column card beside the open sidebar is ~230px wide: check new tools at that width. At a glance under a 420px body puts one figure per line with its label beside it.

## Destinations: administrators open every page unless its gate binds them
`Destinations` lets an administrator open every page, role-tied ones included (PagePanelsTest), but routes have no administrator shortcut. A page whose own policy refuses administrators too is declared with `bindsAdministrators: true` (today the risk library, MEAL Manager slot only, D-61); without it the sidebar, Ask and Desk cards offered a page that answered 403. Card rows pass the record's other-language name as `row(..., words:)` so a card's search finds it in either language.

## Page cards draw records in their own shape; quick actions group first
Every PageCard declares `layout()` (list, agenda, timeline, meter, rank, ledger, people, feed, coded, tagged, numbered, bank) and builds rows with `shaped($row, lead:, end:, bar:)`; the one renderer (⚡page) and desk-workspace.css draw them. Rows stay a vertical list so desk-filters.js can fit them; never lay records out as a tile grid. On narrow cards a pill gives way wherever colour already says the state (bar, tagged edge, date leaf). A zero count draws an empty track. In the Customize sheet, `a-…` items are their own "Quick actions" group (`Panels::readyKindOf` → 'actions'), listed first. While a Desk tab is in front, desk-tabs.js `syncChrome()` moves the sidebar's `dk-on`/`dk-has-on` and the crumb to the tab's page and restores them on the Desk. The collapsed rail never reserves room for the sidebar "+".

## Each page card has its own picture; Desk services are per-request
Every PageCard returns `hero()`: a picture of its page drawn by `x-desk.card-hero` in place of the figures strip (ruler, ring, years, pyramid, tally, gauge, flow, weeks, money, coins, faces, pulse, podium, bubbles, waffle, dots, tags). No two cards share a kind (PageCardsTest checks); the figures stay as `sr-only` for screen readers. Destinations, Panels, Shortcuts and PageCards are `scoped` singletons; `Panels::availableTo`/`itemsForRoutes` are memoized per user + current permissions, and `layoutFor` is never memoized (a save in the same request must be seen). The Ask index is cached 10 min under a key of the viewer's pages, roles, permissions and locale; `placed` is always fresh. `x-desk.icon` draws from the page's sprite (`<use href="#dk-sym-…">`), so the sprite is rendered on every app page, tabs included.

## Desk tabs are always on; full page opens a browser tab; drops open tabs
User decisions 2026-11-10: the "Open pages in desk tabs" switch is removed for good (no avatar-menu item, no Ask command; desk-tabs.js clears the old hc-desk-tabs-off key). ## Quick access: filled only by dragging from the Ask bar, saved on the account
User decisions 2026-11-10: the Desk's Quick access button sits beside the Needs you figures (pages/desk/_quick-access) and is hidden until it holds at least one entry. Entries are added only by dragging an Ask page or action row onto it; while carrying, a dashed drop target stands in for the button. They are saved on users.desk_quick_access as Ask row keys (go:<route>, new:<action>), in order, max 12, and resolved through App\Support\Desk\QuickAccess against AskCommands, so lost access hides an entry without erasing it. The dropdown reorders by dragging rows and removes with a hover ×. Clicking a page opens it as a Desk tab; clicking an action opens its form.

## The Desk holds only panels and page cards; buttons always go to Quick access
User decision 2026-11-10 (supersedes "Every page and action can be on the Desk" for buttons and the Quick access max-12 / drag-only note): a button (`a-…` action, `g-…` page, Shortcuts) never sits in users.desk_layout. The Desk's storeLayout() is the single gate: any button in a new arrangement goes to QuickAccess::addItem (max 20) and the rest is flushed; Panels::layoutFor drops buttons; mount() moves buttons left in an old desk_layout into Quick access once. addItem/addRoute/dropItem, ToggleDeskItemController (answers "quick": true), the Customize Buttons tab ($wire.addQuickItem, hides ids in data-quick-items), Ask "add" rows ("Add “X” to quick access"; AskCommands placed includes QuickAccess::itemIds) and sidebar drags of button-only pages (dk-carry-quick drop target) all land there. Ready layouts hold no buttons.

## Empty panels fold by themselves and open for a visit on click
User decision 2026-11-10: a panel with nothing to show (x-desk.panel :empty, rendered as data-empty; not a filter or search that found nothing) folds into a tile by itself on My desk — the My to-do list included (⚡todo passes :empty="$empty"). Clicking an empty folded tile opens it for this visit only (deskPanel.peek() → Alpine store deskFlags.peeked, skipped by desk-fold.js foldOf); nothing is saved. Once it holds something it stays open. Fold changes glide via desk-grid.js flip() inside applyFolds(); the first layout is instant.
